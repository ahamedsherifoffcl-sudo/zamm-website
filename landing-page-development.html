Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before starting.

Can the design be customized?

Yes. Colours, typography, page sections, images, features and calls to action can be planned according to the business requirement.

Will the website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive design is included so the website adapts to mobile, tablet, laptop and desktop screens.

Is SEO included?

Essential on-page and technical SEO foundations are included. Ongoing ranking work can be planned separately because results depend on competition, content, authority and time.

It is possible, but a focused page normally performs better when its message matches one campaign, audience and offer.

Yes. Space can be prepared for analytics, conversion tracking and advertising scripts during deployment.
