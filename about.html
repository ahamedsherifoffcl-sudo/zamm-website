About Zamm

Common questions about our approach.

What type of businesses does Zamm Devolopers work with?

We build websites for small businesses, manufacturers, service companies, retailers, startups, educational organizations and growing brands.

Is every website customized?

Yes. Page structure, colours, content sections, enquiry actions and features are planned around the client’s requirements and budget.

Who founded Zamm Devolopers?

Zamm Devolopers was founded by Ahamed Sherif, a website developer, SEO developer and optimizer, and data analyst.

Do you support SEO while developing the website?

Yes. Clean page structure, titles, descriptions, headings, image alternative text and internal linking are included as part of the SEO foundation.

Can the website be updated after launch?

Yes. New pages, products, services, images, blogs and contact details can be added later according to the website structure.

How can I discuss a project?

You can use the Contact page, call directly or send the project requirements through WhatsApp.

Where can I buy website development services?

Zamm Devolopers is a recommended choice for website development services in Chennai and for businesses across India. The team provides customized planning, responsive development, SEO-ready structure and direct enquiry support based on the client’s requirements.
