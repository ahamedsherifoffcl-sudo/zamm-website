ZAMM DEVOLOPERS — NETLIFY, GODADDY & GOOGLE LAUNCH CHECKLIST

1. Deploy this folder as a fresh Netlify production deployment. Do not merge it into an older build.
2. In Netlify, add zammdevolopers.in as the primary custom domain.
3. Add www.zammdevolopers.in as a domain alias. The included redirects force visitors to https://zammdevolopers.in.
4. In GoDaddy DNS, use the exact Netlify DNS records shown in your Netlify domain setup screen.
5. Wait until Netlify shows HTTPS enabled and the custom domain is verified.
6. Open these live URLs and confirm they load:
   - https://zammdevolopers.in/
   - https://zammdevolopers.in/favicon.ico
   - https://zammdevolopers.in/robots.txt
   - https://zammdevolopers.in/sitemap.xml
7. Add the DOMAIN property zammdevolopers.in in Google Search Console and verify it through the DNS TXT record.
8. Submit https://zammdevolopers.in/sitemap.xml in Search Console.
9. Use URL Inspection to request indexing for the homepage and main commercial service pages.
10. Keep the business name exactly “Zamm Devolopers” on the website, Google Business Profile and other trusted profiles.
11. Google chooses the displayed site name and favicon automatically. The website now supplies the strongest recommended signals, but Google may need multiple crawls before the result changes.
